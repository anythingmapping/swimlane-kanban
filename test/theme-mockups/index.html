Geo-Kanban Theme Mockups

Click any card to open the full mockup. Each shows the same kanban board with different visual treatments.

1. Atlantis Luminous

Blue-teal holographic panels, cyan glow borders, frosted glass columns. The refined Ancient tech look.

2. Goa'uld Imperial

Obsidian black with polished gold accents. Sharp angles, solid surfaces. Egyptian authority.

3. Abydos Sandstone

Warm desert tones. Cards are lighter sandstone tablets against dark columns. The original movie feel.

4. Ori Flame

Deep crimson-orange ember glow. Fire-touched surfaces with warm amber highlights. Hallowed are the Ori.

5. SGC Military

Cheyenne Mountain bunker. Dark olive green, phosphor green text, monospace type. CRT terminal aesthetic.

6. Kung Fu Panda — Jade Palace

Jade emerald, ocean blue, teal cyan, indigo. Dark ocean base with carved-lacquer buttons and green/blue variety.

7. Tectonic Drift

Jagged plate edges with magma orange glow. Charcoal base, lava red and basalt grey accents.

8. Bathymetric Depth

Ocean depth zones with wavy column edges. Turquoise shallows fading to deep navy abyss.

9. Topographic Survey

Contour line map aesthetic. Brown-green altitude gradient with elevation marker cards.

10. Magnetic Field Lines

Skewed swimlanes following field curves. Aurora purple, plasma cyan, electric blue glow.

11. River Delta

Organic flowing shapes with generous curves. River blue, delta green, silt brown. Sediment-style cards.

12. Geological Strata

Rock layer textures per swimlane. Limestone, granite, obsidian. Cards as embedded fossils.

13. Nautical Dead Reckoning

Old nautical chart with lat/long grid overlay. Parchment, navy, compass gold waypoints.

14. Aurora Borealis

Polar night with star-field. Undulating aurora curtain edges. Green, purple, pink translucent glow.

15. Desert Dunes

Sinusoidal sand dune edges. Ochre-amber warmth with oasis teal. Half-buried tablet cards.

16. Cave System

Jagged cavern edges with stalactites. Bioluminescent cyan, crystal purple. Glowing crystal cards.
